Hair Care Routine

Your personalized daily and weekly routine

🌞 Morning Care

  • Gently comb your hair.
  • Avoid excessive heat styling.
  • Keep hair protected from rough handling.

🚿 Wash Day Routine

  • Use a suitable shampoo for your scalp.
  • Clean the scalp gently.
  • Apply conditioner to the hair lengths.
  • Rinse thoroughly.

🌙 Night Care

  • Gently detangle your hair.
  • Avoid sleeping with very tight hairstyles.
  • Keep hair clean and comfortable overnight.

💆 Scalp Care

  • Avoid aggressive scratching.
  • Keep the scalp clean.
  • Use hair products according to their instructions.

📅 Weekly Care

  • Follow a regular hair-washing routine.
  • Use a conditioning treatment when appropriate.
  • Check your hair and scalp for changes.
